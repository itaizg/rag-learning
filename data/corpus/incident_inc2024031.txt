HELIOS ROBOTICS — INCIDENT REPORT
Incident ID: INC-2024-031
Title: HeliosArm V2 Joint 4 Torque Sensor Erratic Readings at High Temperature
Severity: High
Status: Mitigated (firmware patch pending)
Date Reported: 2024-08-14
Date Closed: Open
Reported By: Hana Völker (Field Service, EMEA)
Assigned To: Sasha Ivanova (Firmware), Marcus Webb (Mechanical)
Customer: AutoFab GmbH, Stuttgart, Germany

DESCRIPTION
During a sustained high-speed welding cycle at AutoFab GmbH's plant (ambient temperature ~42°C), Joint 4 of two HeliosArm V2 units (serial numbers HR-ARM-V2-001842 and HR-ARM-V2-001843) reported torque sensor readings that spiked to ±250% of expected values at joint speeds above 130°/s. This triggered protective stops approximately 3–5 times per hour, halting the welding line and causing an estimated €18,000 per day in lost production.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
Investigation by Marcus Webb and Sasha Ivanova (2024-08-22):
1. The Joint 4 torque sensor (OEM part TRQ-EXT-J4) uses an analogue signal path that is susceptible to thermal noise above 38°C due to insufficient thermal isolation from the joint's main bearing housing.
2. At speeds >130°/s, the sensor sampling frequency (8 kHz) creates aliasing artefacts that are amplified by the noise floor increase at elevated temperatures.
3. The firmware's noise filter (Butterworth, 2nd order, 500 Hz cutoff) is not aggressive enough to suppress these artefacts.

IMMEDIATE MITIGATION (applied 2024-08-23)
- HeliosMaster safety configuration updated to limit Joint 4 max speed to 120°/s on both units.
- AutoFab GmbH production resumed with ~8% cycle time increase.

LONG-TERM REMEDIATION
1. Firmware patch FW-V2-2.3.2: increase filter order to 4th, reduce cutoff to 300 Hz, add temperature-compensation coefficient. Estimated release: 2024-12-01.
2. Hardware fix (V2.3.x units only): add thermal pad HR-THRM-J4-PAD to J4 sensor mount. Kit part: HR-FIX-J4-THERM. Field upgrade can be done by FSE in ~90 minutes.
3. HeliosArm V3 (PROJ-TITAN) redesigns this joint with in-house FTS-HR-01 sensor and improved thermal management — long-term permanent fix.

AFFECTED UNITS
All HeliosArm V2 units with firmware < FW-V2-2.3.2 operating at ambient temperature >38°C and Joint 4 programmed speed >120°/s. Estimated affected fleet: ~47 units worldwide.

COMMUNICATION
Customer notification sent 2024-09-01 (all HeliosArm V2 customers).
Advisory bulletin: BULL-2024-007.
