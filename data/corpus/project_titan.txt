HELIOS ROBOTICS — PROJECT BRIEF
Project Name: Project Titan
Project Code: PROJ-TITAN
Status: Active (Phase 2 of 3)
Start Date: 2024-01-15
Target Completion: 2025-06-30
Project Owner: Dr. Amara Nwosu (VP Engineering)
Lead Engineer: Lena Bergström

OBJECTIVE
Develop the HeliosArm V3, the next-generation 7-DOF robotic arm with integrated vision, increased payload (25 kg), and a new force-torque sensor suite designed in-house (replacing the OEM sensor used in V2).

SCOPE
- New kinematic chain: 7 DOF (additional wrist twist joint vs V2's 6 DOF)
- Payload: 25 kg (V2: 12 kg), reach 1,600 mm (V2: 1,350 mm)
- Integrated wrist camera: Intel RealSense D435i (part HR-CAM-D435I)
- In-house force-torque sensor: FTS-HR-01 (6-axis, designed by Dr. Isaac Müller's team)
- Improved thermal management for joint 4 (addresses known INC-2024-031 issue)
- Compatible with HC-400 controller (firmware upgrade) and new HC-500 controller (PROJ-CTRL-500)

PHASE STATUS
Phase 1 (Design & Prototyping): COMPLETE (2024-01-15 – 2024-07-30)
  - Kinematic design frozen
  - FTS-HR-01 prototype validated in lab
  - 3 prototype arms built

Phase 2 (Validation & Testing): IN PROGRESS (2024-08-01 – 2025-01-31)
  - Endurance testing: 500,000 cycle target (currently at 180,000 cycles as of 2024-11-01)
  - EMC testing: scheduled 2024-12
  - ISO 10218-1 certification: in progress with TÜV Rheinland

Phase 3 (Industrialisation): PENDING
  - Manufacturing process setup
  - Service documentation
  - Partner training

KEY RISKS
1. FTS-HR-01 sensor shows 0.5% drift after 200,000 cycles (under investigation by Dr. Isaac Müller, Jira HENG-4521)
2. HC-500 controller (dependency) behind schedule by ~6 weeks (PROJ-CTRL-500 status: Phase 1 delayed)
3. RealSense D435I supply chain: lead time increased to 18 weeks (procurement tracking PR-2024-088)
