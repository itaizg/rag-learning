HELIOS ROBOTICS — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Model: HeliosArm V2 Industrial Robotic Arm
Part Number: HR-ARM-V2-6DOF
SKU: HRA-2024-001
Version: 2.3.1
Last Updated: 2024-10-15

OVERVIEW
The HeliosArm V2 is a 6-degree-of-freedom industrial robotic arm designed for precision assembly, welding, and material handling in manufacturing environments. It builds upon the V1 platform with a 40% increase in payload capacity and a new torque-sensing safety layer.

PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Payload Capacity: 12 kg (rated), 15 kg (peak, <10 seconds)
Reach: 1,350 mm (fully extended)
Repeatability: ±0.03 mm
Weight (arm only): 38 kg
Mounting: Floor, ceiling, or wall (requires adapter plate AP-WALL-02)
Footprint: 220 mm diameter base

JOINT SPECIFICATIONS
Joint 1 (base rotation): ±170°, max 120°/s
Joint 2 (shoulder): ±130°, max 100°/s
Joint 3 (elbow): ±150°, max 110°/s
Joint 4 (wrist roll): ±180°, max 150°/s
Joint 5 (wrist pitch): ±120°, max 150°/s
Joint 6 (wrist yaw): ±360° continuous, max 180°/s

ELECTRICAL
Input Voltage: 200–240 V AC, 3-phase, 50/60 Hz
Peak Power Consumption: 2,800 W
Standby Power: 45 W
Communication Interfaces: EtherCAT (primary), Modbus TCP, PROFINET (optional module: HR-NET-PFN)
Emergency Stop: Hardwired E-stop + software watchdog (IEC 62061 SIL 2 certified)

CONTROLLER
Compatible Controller: HeliosControl HC-400 (sold separately, part HC400-CTRL)
Software: HeliosMaster v5.x or higher
Programming Modes: Teach pendant, offline simulation (HeliosSim), ROS 2 compatible (ROS2-PKG-ARM)

SAFETY FEATURES
- Torque sensing on all 6 joints (ISO/TS 15066 compliant for collaborative operation)
- Protective stop category 0 and 1
- Joint position limits (hardware + software)
- Speed monitoring zones configurable via HeliosMaster

ENVIRONMENTAL
Operating Temperature: 5°C to 45°C
Storage Temperature: -20°C to 60°C
Humidity: 20%–80% RH (non-condensing)
IP Rating: IP54 (standard), IP65 with optional cover kit HR-COVER-IP65

ACCESSORIES & RELATED PARTS
- HR-EE-GRIP-01: Standard pneumatic gripper (payload 3 kg, stroke 50 mm)
- HR-EE-WELD-02: MIG welding end-effector kit
- HR-CAB-5M: 5-metre control cable
- HR-CAB-10M: 10-metre control cable
- AP-WALL-02: Wall-mount adapter plate
- HR-COVER-IP65: IP65 protection cover kit

KNOWN ISSUES (see incident reports INC-2024-031, INC-2024-047)
Joint 4 torque sensor may report erratic readings above 130°/s at ambient temperatures exceeding 40°C. Firmware patch FW-V2-2.3.2 (pending release) addresses this. Interim mitigation: limit Joint 4 speed to 120°/s in HeliosMaster safety configuration.

WARRANTY
24 months from date of shipment. Excludes damage caused by operation outside rated specifications.
