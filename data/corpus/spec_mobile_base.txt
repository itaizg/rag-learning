HELIOS ROBOTICS — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Model: HeliosBase M1 Autonomous Mobile Robot Base
Part Number: HR-MOB-M1-AMR
SKU: HRM-2024-010
Version: 1.5.0
Last Updated: 2024-09-01

OVERVIEW
The HeliosBase M1 is an omnidirectional autonomous mobile robot (AMR) base designed for indoor logistics and last-metre delivery in warehouses, hospitals, and manufacturing floors. It can carry up to 120 kg and integrates with the HeliosArm V2 via the HR-DOCK-ARM adapter (part HR-DOCK-ARM-M1) to form a complete mobile manipulation platform.

PHYSICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Payload Capacity: 120 kg
Max Speed (loaded): 1.2 m/s
Max Speed (unloaded): 1.8 m/s
Battery: 48 V, 40 Ah LiFePO4 (part HR-BAT-48V40)
Battery Life: 8 hours typical (120 kg load, 1.0 m/s average)
Charge Time: 2.5 hours (fast charger HR-CHRG-FC), 5 hours (standard)
Weight (base only): 85 kg
Footprint: 700 mm × 600 mm
Height: 300 mm (without payload platform)

DRIVE SYSTEM
Drive Type: 4-wheel omni (Mecanum wheels)
Wheel Diameter: 200 mm
Turning Radius: 0 mm (in-place rotation)
Obstacle Avoidance: 360° LIDAR (SICK TIM-781, part HR-LIDAR-TIM781) + 4 ultrasonic sensors
Navigation: SLAM + map-based with dynamic re-planning (ROS 2 Navigation Stack)

COMMUNICATION
Interfaces: Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), Gigabit Ethernet, CAN bus (internal)
Fleet Management: HeliosFleet v2.x API (REST + WebSocket)
Emergency Stop: Hardware button + software watchdog + remote E-stop via fleet API

SENSORS
LIDAR: SICK TIM-781 (mounted front-top)
Ultrasonic: ×4 (front, rear, left, right bumper zones)
IMU: 6-axis (internal, non-replaceable)
Odometry: Wheel encoders (1024 PPR) + IMU fusion

INTEGRATION
HeliosArm V2 Mount: HR-DOCK-ARM-M1 adapter plate (rated 40 kg arm weight + 12 kg payload)
Power Pass-Through: 200–240 V AC output for mounted arm controller (max 3,500 W)

SAFETY
ISO 3691-4 compliant (industrial trucks — automated)
Laser safety zone: configurable via HeliosFleet (warning zone 1.5 m, protective zone 0.5 m default)
Emergency deceleration: full stop within 0.4 m at 1.2 m/s

ENVIRONMENTAL
Operating Temperature: 5°C to 40°C
Humidity: 20%–80% RH
Floor Conditions: Dry, flat indoor; max slope 5°; max threshold 10 mm

ACCESSORIES
- HR-DOCK-ARM-M1: HeliosArm V2 mounting adapter
- HR-BAT-48V40: Replacement battery pack
- HR-CHRG-FC: Fast charger (48 V, 20 A)
- HR-LIDAR-TIM781: Replacement LIDAR unit

KNOWN ISSUES
Battery calibration drift can occur after >500 charge cycles, causing the state-of-charge indicator to read ~10% high. Addressed in firmware FW-M1-1.5.1. See also incident report INC-2024-019.
